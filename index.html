Portfolio project · Customer analytics

Maven Airline Loyalty Analysis

How does flight activity vary by season and loyalty tier?

27.9% more flights in 2018

Flights rose from 223,262 in 2017 to 285,546 in 2018. July was the busiest month in both years, with July activity increasing by 44.2%.

Approach: Combined customer flight activity and loyalty history in Power BI, with DAX measures, year and enrollment-type filters, and detailed views by time, location, profile, and tier.

Dataset period: January 2017–December 2018.

Interpretation & next step

Examine the customer groups behind the summer peak to inform seasonal loyalty communications. Growth is calculated from annual totals; it does not establish promotion impact. Tier reconciliation and membership definitions are documented in the case study.
